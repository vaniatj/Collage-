The Future

By Vania Trujillo

This collage is a visual story that brings together different moments and symbols to represent a bigger message. I chose these specific images because they capture a feeling that a single photo couldn't show on its own. By layering these pieces together, I am trying to show how different parts of life or history connect to create a meaningful whole. This project is a reflection of my creative journey and the social message I want to share with the world.
